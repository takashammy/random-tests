# Locked design — Soft Uncanny × Luminary

This is the visual we will build against. Do not revert to Luminary newsprint, Clay Sanctuary still-life, or pictorial emblems (cups, doors, keys, moons, feathers).

## Mix

- **Look:** Soft Uncanny — Surrealism × Pointillism.
- **Chassis:** Luminary’s editorial UI (poster grid, vertical spines, volume index, tracked badges, numbered modules, bilingual essay, footer).
- **Rule:** type, grain, and structure only. No photographs, illustrations, or objects.

## Palette and type

- Field: pointillist grain in mist, grain-blue `#7A90A8`, dusk rose `#C9898A`, pollen `#E6D48A`. Not newsprint `#FAF6EE`.
- Ink: warm stone `#3A3532`.
- Titles and prose: readable serif (Newsreader-class for English; Song/Ming for Chinese at the same rank).
- Labels: uppercase grotesque, wide tracking (`DAILY READ | RELATIONSHIPS`).

## Luminary structure (keep)

- Inset 1px frame + outer registration bleed.
- Left ~58px spine: vertical-rl English, upright Chinese.
- Volume numeral + category badge, hairline split.
- `DURATION` and `THEME` spines, pull-quote, core thesis.
- Modules `01` `02` `03` (`04` for practice).
- Footer: Read · Library · Calendar · You. Reader also has a next-volume line.
