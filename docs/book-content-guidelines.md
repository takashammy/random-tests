# Book content guidelines

Use this whenever writing or rewriting Keep / Luminary-style volumes. Pair with repo `.cursorrules` for Chinese (信、达、雅; two-step translate-then-polish; 简体书评 tone).

## Highlights and extra points

Each summary point starts with a **quote or key point**, then an **easy layman explanation**.

1. **Lead:** a short quote from the book, or a crisp key claim.
2. **Explain:** everyday language — what it means in a kitchen, a text thread, a walk home. No jargon unless immediately unpacked.
3. **Chinese:** translate the *explanation* (and a faithful version of the quote/key point). Do not invent a new argument in Chinese. Follow `.cursorrules`.

English and Chinese should be the same idea, twice.

## Eastern resonance

Does **not** have to be classical Chinese. Contemporary Chinese, modern essays, or living practice texts are fine if they truly resonate. Classical sources remain welcome when they fit. Commentary should connect the Western point to that text in warm, intimate editorial voice.

## Behavior practice

Make it easy to actually do. No motivational barrier.

- Solo, one sitting, a few minutes.
- Concrete: one action, one sentence to write, or one pause — not a lifestyle overhaul.
- No gym-bro streaks, no “if you really loved them you’d…”
- Optional journal line, never a performance.

## Also locked

- Original editorial, not licensed summaries.
- Luminary shape: thesis, 3 highlights, extras, resonance, one practice.
- Books are independent ideas — no volume numbers.
- Navigation chrome English-only; bilingual editorial EN + 繁/简.
